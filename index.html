Under Review • 2025 Springer Cluster Computing LLM Multi-Task Learning

Adapting Large Language Models via Multi-Task Learning for Mental Health Problem, Emotion, and Sentiment Detection

Amir Rafiq Bhat, Rahim Dehkharghani, Zeki Erdem

This study introduces an empirical framework using Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT/LoRA) on open-source foundation models (Llama 3.1) to jointly model mental disorders, fine-grained emotions, and sentiment from social media narratives. It specifically formulates a logging architecture to quantify and prevent catastrophic forgetting, demonstrating that multi-task adaptation surpasses single-task models without sacrificing baseline safety or general reasoning capabilities.
